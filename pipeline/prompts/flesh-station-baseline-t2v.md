A three-soldier containment team withdraws on foot down a space-station corridor that has
been consumed by living tissue. Ropey red-brown sinew and thick tendrils arch over the
ceiling and walls, closing the passage into a wet ribbed tunnel; membranous sacs and
clustered nodules bulge between them, chitinous limbs jut from the walls at head height,
and ichor beads and drips from everything. Panels of the original station — pale wall
plating, a pressure-door frame, metal floor grating — still show through the growth. Cold
desaturated grey-teal ambience against deep maroon and raw-pink flesh; the far end of the
corridor falls off into black.

Leading the group, deep in frame, is a massive four-legged combat robot built in the shape
of a sabre-toothed big cat, twice a soldier's height at the shoulder — segmented gunmetal
armour plating, weathered and scuffed, heavy hydraulic limbs ending in three-toed claw
feet, a skull-like feline head with bared sabre fangs and a single glowing cyan optic, two
long rotary cannons mounted along its flanks with muzzle clusters projecting past the
shoulders, boxed equipment modules over the rear haunches, orange-yellow hazard chevrons
breaking up the grey. It advances at a slow deliberate walk — one heavy foot at a time, the
whole chassis settling on each step, hydraulics hissing, its head sweeping the way ahead.

The soldiers are anthropomorphic feline troopers: lean athletic build, digitigrade legs
ending in paw feet, long tails, full-face armoured cat helmets in smooth grey plate with
upright pointed ears, large black lens eyes and a short muzzle respirator, worn sealed.
Dark charcoal armoured bodysuits with sculpted chest plates, harness webbing and belt
pouches, thigh and shin plating, clawed gauntlets, a restrained orange-tan chevron on one
shoulder; black bullpup combat shotguns. Two of them follow close behind the machine,
weapons low, one steadying the other by the shoulder, boots finding the grating carefully
through the wet growth. The third walks backwards nearest the camera, shotgun shouldered
and aimed past the lens into the dark corridor behind, checking over her shoulder once to
see where she is stepping before turning back to cover the way they came. Nobody hurries.
Nobody speaks. Helmets stay sealed.

Photoreal grimdark military science fiction. Handheld camera at chest height, travelling
backwards with the squad so the group holds frame and the dark tunnel behind them stays
open past the rear soldier. Wide lens, shallow depth of field, heavy film grain, hard
helmet-lamp beams cutting through hanging moisture, wet specular highlights on the flesh.

the corridor, a single long reverberating groan. No music.
