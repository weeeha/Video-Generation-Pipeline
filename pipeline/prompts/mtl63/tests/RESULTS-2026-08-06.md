## Recommended workflow

1. Use T2V only for visual discovery and broad establishing ideas.
2. Create a face-free or ModelArk-trusted master frame for every recurring character or robot.
3. Use R2V to establish a new shot from character and location packs.
4. Once a shot is approved, use its original last frame as the next I2V first frame.
5. Use first+last I2V for exact blocking, entrances, exits, and match transitions.
6. Use V2V selectively when preserving source motion is worth approximately twice the standard test cost.
7. Keep native ambience, but record or dub precise dialogue in post.
