# Mtl63 Restaurant Service Unit

**One-line:** a weathered pre-collapse restaurant service robot that continues its table-service routine in an abandoned Montreal dining room.

## Canonical description

A compact vintage restaurant service robot with a rounded cream-and-olive cylindrical body, a dark horizontal head band, one rectangular amber display lamp, weathered enamel, two articulated metal arms, a wheeled base, and an integrated glassware tray. Its movements are careful, economical, and slightly aged rather than cute, aggressive, or humanoid.

## Don'ts

- No humans, faces, skeletons, portraits, or additional robots.
- Do not add eyes, a mouth, legs, weapons, or a humanoid face.
- Keep the glass intact and the arm joints mechanically plausible.
- No readable text, logos, neon accents, or watermark.
