# Mtl63 Rooftop Garden

**One-line:** a rain-soaked improvised rooftop garden and workshop overlooking a partially reclaimed but recognizable Montreal.

## Canonical description

A broad Montreal rooftop twenty-five years after technological collapse: shallow rainwater ponds, wet concrete, raised vegetable planters, patched solar panels, repaired cables, a small improvised workshop, and carefully organized salvage. Downtown towers and Mount Royal remain recognizable beyond the roof, with selective vine growth and rooftop trees rather than total destruction. Cool blue-grey post-rain morning light comes from camera left while a narrow warm break in the clouds and amber workshop lamps sit toward frame right.

## Don'ts

- Never turn the city into a tropical jungle, desert, neon cyberpunk skyline, or uniformly destroyed ruin.
- Keep Montreal geography and concrete architecture plausible.
- Preserve the cool exterior light, wet surfaces, and restrained amber practical lights.
- No readable signs, logos, HUD elements, or watermarks.
