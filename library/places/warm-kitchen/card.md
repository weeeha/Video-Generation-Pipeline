# Warm Kitchen

**One-line:** the bright stylized-3D family kitchen — Nova's home turf and the default cozy domestic location.

## Canonical description

A bright modern family kitchen in a stylized 3D-animated look: cream shaker cabinets,
white subway-tile backsplash, stainless range with hood, white farmhouse sink, warm wooden
countertops and floor, a small island with wooden stools, potted herbs by the window. Warm
golden morning light streams in from a window on the left. Warm amber-and-cream palette.

## Don'ts

- Window/light always camera-LEFT; don't let generations flip it.
- No cold/blue lighting, no granite or dark-modern finishes.
- Keep it lived-in but tidy — herbs, wooden boards, fruit bowl; no clutter, no branding.
