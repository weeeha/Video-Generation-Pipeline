Late 1980s Montreal, March melt, overcast afternoon. A colossal abandoned spherical machine, dented grey riveted hull, torn blue tarp, cables spilling out, lies across the collapsed ramps of the Turcot interchange. Wet black asphalt with grey slush, a line of orange detour cones, a red traffic light. A 1991 Chevrolet Caprice taxi idles at the red light in the foreground, seen from behind, small in frame. Light sleet drifting, fog dissolving the distant city and power pylons. Low horizon, eye-level wide shot, 28mm, deep focus, desaturated grey-blue palette with the orange cones as the only warm accent. Very slow push-in, no camera shake. The machine is completely still. Nobody reacts. No explosions, no fire, no sparks, no neon.
