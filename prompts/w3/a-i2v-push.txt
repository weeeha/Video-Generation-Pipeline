Very slow push-in at eye level, no camera shake. The colossal crashed dome machine lying across the collapsed Turcot interchange is completely still: no lights, no sparks, no movement. Light sleet drifts across frame, grey fog slowly moves behind the machine and dissolves the distant city. The taxi idles at the red light, a thin wisp of exhaust rising. Traffic lights stay red. Puddles ripple slightly in the sleet. Quiet, melancholic, overcast March afternoon. No fast motion, no new objects.
