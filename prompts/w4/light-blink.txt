Locked-off camera at eye level, the frame does not move at all. The giant grain-silo walker machine standing in the frozen river is completely still. The figure on the quay stands still, seen from behind, holding a thermos. Quiet, overcast winter afternoon. No fast motion, no new objects, nobody reacts. No snow falling and the fog does not move. The only change: the small red light on top of the machine's tower blinks slowly on and off, and its faint glow reflects on the ice.
