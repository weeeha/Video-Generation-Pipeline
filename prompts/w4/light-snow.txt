Locked-off camera at eye level, the frame does not move at all. The giant grain-silo walker machine standing in the frozen river is completely still. The figure on the quay stands still, seen from behind, holding a thermos. Quiet, overcast winter afternoon. No fast motion, no new objects, nobody reacts. Light snow falls gently and steadily, small flakes drifting slowly down and slightly to the right, the same density for the whole shot. The fog stays as it is.
