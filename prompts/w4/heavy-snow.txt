Locked-off camera at eye level, the frame does not move at all. The giant grain-silo walker machine standing in the frozen river is completely still. The figure on the quay stands still, seen from behind, holding a thermos. Quiet, overcast winter afternoon. No fast motion, no new objects, nobody reacts. Heavy snow falls steadily, large flakes blowing from left to right, the same direction and density for the whole shot, partly hiding the machine. The fog stays as it is.
